What Jankrats is

Jankrats is a free, non-commercial fan-made tool for tracking a Riftbound Trading Card Game collection, building decks, running local tournaments, and comparing collections with friends. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Riot Games — see the notice above.

Your account

You can use most of Jankrats without an account. Signing in (with Google or Discord) unlocks syncing your collection/decks across devices and the Friends/social features. You're responsible for keeping access to your own account, and for anything posted under it.

Play nice. You keep ownership of anything you post — decks, pull photos, comments, your profile. You can delete your own content at any time.
